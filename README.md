CS 370: Current and Emerging Trends in Computer Science

Pirate Intelligent Agent — Project Two Portfolio Reflection

Throughout this course, I gained a better understanding of artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, and how computers can learn to make decisions. One of the most important projects I completed involved developing an intelligent pirate agent that could navigate a maze and locate treasure. This project allowed me to apply the concepts I learned throughout the course while strengthening my programming and problem-solving skills.

For this project, I was provided with starter code that included the maze environment, supporting classes, and portions of the neural network implementation. My responsibility was to complete and troubleshoot the training process so the pirate agent could learn how to make decisions and improve its performance. I used Python and Jupyter Notebook to work with deep Q-learning, action selection, rewards, and the training process. Although I experienced challenges with coding errors and understanding how different parts of the program worked together, I learned how to identify problems, make corrections, and test my work.

This experience helped me recognize the important role computer scientists play in today’s society. Computer scientists develop software, create algorithms, analyze information, and design technological solutions that help individuals and organizations solve problems. Their work is important because technology continues to influence healthcare, education, transportation, business, and many other industries. I learned that computer science involves more than writing code. It also requires creativity, critical thinking, and the ability to develop solutions that make technology more effective and useful.

Throughout this project, I also developed a better understanding of how to approach problems as a computer scientist. I learned the importance of identifying the problem, breaking it into smaller steps, and testing different solutions. When my code did not work correctly, I had to review the instructions, examine the errors, and determine what changes were necessary. This process taught me that successful programming requires patience, attention to detail, and a willingness to continue learning even when challenges arise.

Another important lesson I gained from this course involves the ethical responsibilities of computer scientists. As a future software engineer, I believe it is important to develop technology that is reliable, secure, and respectful of the people who use it. Computer scientists have a responsibility to protect users’ personal information, consider fairness, and ensure that technology does not create unnecessary harm. They also have a responsibility to organizations to maintain professional standards, protect confidential information, and communicate honestly about technical problems. As artificial intelligence continues to advance, developers must carefully consider how automated decisions could affect individuals and organizations.

Overall, completing the pirate intelligent agent project helped me gain valuable experience with artificial intelligence, neural networks, reinforcement learning, and Python programming. It also strengthened my confidence in troubleshooting technical problems and applying classroom knowledge to practical situations. As I continue working toward a career in software engineering, I plan to build on these skills and continue expanding my knowledge of emerging technologies. Including this project in my GitHub portfolio will allow future employers to see my technical abilities, problem-solving skills, and commitment to professional growth.
